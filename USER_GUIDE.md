# Luanda Feeds Centre - User Guide

## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Launch the application
2. Enter your username and password
3. Select your role (Cashier or Manager)
4. Click "Login"

**Note**: If you are a superuser, you can select any role when logging in.

### Understanding the Dashboard

After login, you'll see the main dashboard showing:
- Today's transactions (count)
- Today's revenue
- Low stock alerts
- Recent activity

## Point of Sale (POS)

### Making a Sale

1. Click "Open POS" from the dashboard
2. In the search box, type at least 2 characters of the item name
3. Click on the item from the dropdown results
4. Adjust quantity using the + / - buttons
5. Repeat for additional items
6. Enter the amount received from the customer
7. Click "Checkout"

### Receipts

After checkout, you'll see the receipt. To print:
1. Click the "Print Receipt" button
2. The browser print dialog will open
3. Select your printer and click "Print"

**Tip**: Receipts are formatted to fit on a single page for thermal printers.

### Change Calculation

The system automatically calculates change. If the amount paid is less than the total, you'll see an error message.

## Inventory Management

### Adding New Items

1. Navigate to "Manage Inventory"
2. Click "Add New Item"
3. Fill in the details:
   - Item Name (required)
   - Buying Price (required)
   - Selling Price (required)
   - Initial Stock
   - Low Stock Threshold
   - Category
4. Click "Save Item"

### Editing Items

1. Navigate to "Manage Inventory"
2. Find the item in the list
3. Click "Edit" next to the item
4. Update the fields
5. Click "Update Item"

**Note**: Price changes are logged for audit purposes.

### Adjusting Stock

1. Click "Adjust Stock"
2. Search for the item
3. Enter the quantity change:
   - Positive (e.g., +10) to add stock
   - Negative (e.g., -5) to reduce stock
4. Select a reason
5. Add notes if needed
6. Click "Apply Adjustment"

## Price Management

### Updating Prices

1. Click "Price Management" from the menu
2. Search for the item
3. Select "Buying" or "Selling" price
4. Enter the new price
5. Click "Update Price"

### Price History

To view all price changes:
1. Click "View Full Price History"
2. Use pagination to navigate through old changes

## Reports

### Generating Sales Reports

1. Click "Sales Report" from the menu
2. Select start and end dates
3. Click "Generate Report"

### Exporting Reports

After generating a report, you can export it:
- Click "Export PDF" for a formatted PDF report
- Click "Export Excel" for a spreadsheet

## User Management (Managers Only)

### Changing User Passwords

1. Click "Manage Users" from the menu
2. Select the user
3. Enter the new password (minimum 8 characters)
4. Confirm the password
5. Click "Update Password"

## Tips & Best Practices

### Stock Management
- Set low stock thresholds for items you need to reorder
- Check the low stock alert on the dashboard daily
- Adjust stock promptly when deliveries arrive

### Daily Operations
- Start the day by checking the dashboard
- Review any low stock alerts
- Keep receipts filed for record keeping

### Reports
- Run daily sales reports at end of day
- Use date ranges to track performance
- Export to Excel for further analysis

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Find an Item in POS
- Check if the item has stock (items with 0 stock won't appear)
- Verify spelling - try partial matches
- The item might be in a different category

### Checkout Won't Process
- Ensure amount paid >= total
- Check if all items have sufficient stock
- Try refreshing and re-adding cart items

### Can't Access a Menu Item
- You may not have the right role
- Contact your manager for access
